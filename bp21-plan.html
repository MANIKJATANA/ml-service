The problem we're fixing

The app talks about itself from the machine's side — and a few things it says aren't even true.
There are really two steps: (1) find which students are in each photo (face matching), and (2) tell those students their photos are ready. But the app names them in seven different words — the button says "Process photos", the message says "Distribution started", the status says "Processing", the checklist says "Distribute to students", the analytics say "Delivery rate". The word "Distribution" means both steps at once, so an admin presses "Process photos", reads "Distribution started", and reasonably thinks the students were told — but they weren't; that's a separate button. On top of that: a student is told "Only you can see these" — which is false (school staff can see all photos); face matching is never explained to anyone, yet students are asked "Is this you?" and staff correct confidence percentages nobody defined; the "delete student" box doesn't say what it destroys or what it keeps; "Notified 143 students" oversells what's really an in-app flag; and four error messages misdirect (a rate-limit reads as a server outage, a bad email says "Unprocessable Entity", raw error text leaks to users).

The server itself doesn't change — this is all on the app's side.

How we'll check it

Because it's mostly wording, the checks are: the full frontend gate (types + lint + build) stays green, a review confirms the sweep is complete (no stray "Distribution"/"Process" left behind, one grammar everywhere), and a walk-through of each surface. For the error slice specifically:
  1. Let a session expire on a normal page → it sends you to login, not a dead "Retry".
  2. Submit a bad email → the message names the field and the reason, not "Unprocessable Entity".
  3. Trip the rate limit → "try again in N seconds", not "something went wrong".
  4. Force a server fault → one calm generic message, no raw internals.

Not in this build (noted honestly): no outbound email/WhatsApp — that's the separate parked item; slice 1 just states the in-app truth ("In-app only"). The warmer student look and the "newest photos first" arrival fix are their own later phases (BP20). Backend text/enums are untouched — all labels live in the frontend, so there's no migration and no ML change. After this, the roadmap continues with BP20 (the student arrival moment).
