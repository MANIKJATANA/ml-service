The problem we're fixing

Reviewing matches is guesswork — and the moment to catch a bad one is easy to miss.
When the system isn't sure a face is a match, it asks a teacher to check. But the review screen shows only a name and a percentage — "Is this Priya Sharma? 71%" over a group photo, with no picture of Priya. A teacher personally knows maybe two of their classes out of 800 students, so for most names it's a guess — and a wrong "confirm" sends the wrong photo to the wrong student (the exact mistake the review step exists to prevent). Worse, the review lane is on a separate tab of a separate page: the event screen where you press Announce never mentions it, and auto-announce is on, so uncertain matches reach students the instant matching finishes. And the review lane forgets where you were — open a photo, come back, and you're dumped on "All photos" to re-find "Needs review" all over again.
Your calls (already made): the student-side "This isn't me" safety fix is held for its own separate decision (not in this build); announcing while matches are unreviewed shows a count + a one-click confirm (never a hard block); delivered as one phase.
