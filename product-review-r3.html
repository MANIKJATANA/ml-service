The five that matter most

1 · A forgotten student password destroys the child's photo history CriticalR3-A2-01 · nominated independently by 3 agents

No reset path exists for any role; resend-invite covers only staff/admins. The only remedy — delete & recreate — purges the student's matches (BP8e) and the worker skips completed photos on reprocess, so the history is unrecoverable. At 800 students on paper-slip passwords, this will happen. Fix is small: one endpoint reusing proven BP7c machinery + a login help line. → BP18

2 · A stranded processing job says "Distribution is running" forever CriticalR3-S1-01

A job dead-letters (5 failed deliveries) or is lost → the Process button hides, the API refuses re-enqueue, the DLQ has no consumer, no staleness cue, and the failure half of the pipeline emits zero metrics. Students never get the photos; only out-of-band SQL can unstick it. → BP19

3 · The product neither lands nor measures its last mile HighR3-A4-02 · S6-03 · S3-11

The student's badge says "3 new" → the gallery opens oldest-first with the new photos ~850 tiles down, the banner links nowhere, and the badge itself is fetched once per session (frozen in an open tab). Staff-side, "Delivery rate" measures the announce button — per-event opens and saves exist in the DB and reach no screen. → BP20 + BP23

4 · The product misdescribes itself HighR3-S2-01 · S5-02/03/04

Seven words for one pipeline ("Process" toasts "Distribution started." — while distributing is a different button); "Notified 143 students" means 143 log lines; "Only you can see these" is false against staff view-all; face recognition is explained to no one; the delete dialog hides what it destroys. Almost entirely a strings pass. → BP21

5 · The review loop is under-armed at its decision points HighR3-A3-02 · A3-08 · A4-04

No reference face beside the candidate name; review debt invisible at the announce moment while auto-announce defaults on (uncertain matches go student-visible unreviewed); the student's "This isn't me" is a one-tap, unconfirmed, unrecoverable removal that staff are never told about. → BP22

Verified, not vibes

A pre-review sweep alleged 25 rough edges — verification confirmed 10, partially confirmed 4, and refuted 11 (including four invented UI elements). Also positively confirmed: skeletons, aria-sort, live regions, reduced-motion, table scroll wrappers, and zero unlabeled icon buttons all exist — the floor mostly holds. Its two real breaks: sub-AA 12px data text on every table header (one-token fix) and a calendar with no mobile story.

Proposed roadmap (owner picks — build stays paused)

Recommended order  BP18 → BP19 → BP21 → BP20 → BP22 → BP25 → BP23 → BP24
The two Criticals first (BP18 is small and defuses the product's worst failure mode; BP19 is the other no-workaround path), then the cheapest High (BP21 — almost all strings, halves the trust exposure), then the student moment (BP20) and the teacher loop (BP22); the floor sweep (BP25) slots anywhere. Reorder freely — only BP18/BP19 carry the "these are the Criticals" weighting. Eight phases below in that order.
BP18Account recovery & credential safety kills Critical #1Effort S–M
NowA student forgets their password and no one can help — the only "fix" (delete & re-add the student) erases every photo they were ever matched to, for good.
AfterStaff send a fresh password in one click, the login screen tells a stuck family what to do, and nobody loses their photos just to get back into their account.
How: a /students/{id}/resend-invite endpoint (reuses proven BP7c machinery) · close-guards on the shown-once password dialogs · a resend confirm · a Change-password link · PATCH /schools/{id} for the write-once school record.  Theme J · R3-A2-01/A4-01 (Critical) + 6 more · optional BP18b: session revocation + student disable
BP19Pipeline resilience & stall visibility kills Critical #2Effort MML worker
NowIf photo-matching breaks on an event, it just says "Distribution is running…" forever — staff can't tell it's stuck, can't retry, and no alert fires. The photos never reach anyone.
AfterA stuck event shows as failed with a Retry button, staff can see how long it's been running, and the team gets an alert — instead of hearing about it from a parent.
How: an unstick / re-enqueue path · a dead-letter consumer that marks the event failed · failure metrics (jobs-failed, queue depth, age) · "processing since…" copy · retry-failed uploads.  Theme K · R3-S1-01 (Critical) + 5 more · touches the ML worker, no contract change
BP21Say what it does cheapest HighEffort S–M
NowThe app calls one action five different names, tells students "only you can see these" (staff see everything), and says "Notified 143 students" when no message actually went out.
AfterOne clear word per action, honest privacy wording, and status messages that say exactly what happened — so people can trust what they read.
How: one verb pair (Match / Announce) used everywhere · a true "who can see this" line · a short "how matching works" page · honest delete + audit wording · clearer 401/422/429 errors. Almost all copy.  Theme M · R3-S2-01 + 8 more
BP20The arrival moment HighEffort M
NowA student taps "3 new photos" and lands on pictures from three years ago — the new ones are buried ~850 photos down, and the badge often doesn't even light up.
AfterThe gallery opens on the newest photos, the "new" banner jumps straight to them, the badge stays live, and saved files are named by event and date.
How: newest-first order · a clickable "new photos" banner · a live badge · year/term chip groups · event captions on photos and the download zip. The backend already serves it all.  Theme L · R3-A4-02/03 + 4 more · optional BP20b: warm student look
BP22Review loop, armed HighEffort S–M
NowA teacher checking "Is this Priya? 71%" has no picture of Priya — it's a guess — and uncertain matches can reach students before anyone reviews them.
AfterThe student's enrolled face sits right next to the guess, the "still to review" count sits on the Send button, and a mis-tapped "This isn't me" can be undone.
How: the reference-photo thumbnail in the review tile (endpoint already exists) · a review-debt warning at announce · a deep-linkable review tab · a confirm + undo on "This isn't me".  Theme N · R3-A3-02/08 + A4-04
BP25Floor sweep Medium (1 High)Effort S–M
NowFaint grey table text is hard to read, the calendar is unusable on a phone, and reloading a filtered list throws away where you were.
AfterReadable text everywhere it carries meaning, a calendar that works on mobile, and filters and tabs you can bookmark and share.
How: one contrast fix across every table + metric label · a responsive calendar · focus rings + bigger tap targets · list state saved in the URL · per-page titles · brand/motion polish.  Theme Q · R3-S4-01 (High) + ~11 more
BP23Run it on numbers HighEffort M1 migration
NowThe dashboard says "delivery 100%" when it only means "we pressed Send" — no one can see who actually opened or saved their photos, or whether teachers are even using the app.
AfterReal numbers — who opened, who saved, which schools are stalling, which teachers are active — with a name-list behind every percentage.
How: record who uploaded each photo (the one small migration, before that history is lost) · expose last-login + creator · real open/save rates · accuracy from correction data already collected · a list behind each rate.  Theme O · R3-S6-03/04/06 + 6 more
BP24Two-way doors MediumEffort M
NowTag an event wrong and you can never undo it; a bulk CSV import hides its mistakes until after it's already run.
AfterEvery choice is reversible, and a bad import flags its problems up front with a "download the failed rows" to fix and re-try.
How: clearable event tags · CSV duplicate pre-flagging + skipped-row export · class assignment at CSV scale · honest partial-download messages · roster refresh after an invite.  Theme P · R3-A2-05/11 + 3 more
