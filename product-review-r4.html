⚠ v1 pivot (owner decision, 2026-08-29). No student login in v1 — students don't use the app. Distribution is staff-mediated: a teacher/admin downloads a student's photos and shares them via WhatsApp. So the "blocker" below is no longer outbound email (that original BP26 scope is parked to BP12, post-v1) — it's a small, FE-only feature: staff per-student "Download all" for both roles, the reframed BP26. The in-app announce/"opened" path + the whole student surface are dormant in v1 (shipped, unused). Net: the release blocker got cheap.

The verdict in one line

The staff/admin product is mature and well-built — the design bar is met (Linear chrome delivered, Stripe data density substantially closed), the hard problems are solved (BP18 recovery, BP19 pipeline, BP22 review, BP9/17 scale). The pixels are there. What stands between this and a confident release is not design — it's one strategic blocker and three clusters of operability: v1 distribution needs a staff per-student download; the bulk affordances stop one step short; the audit log answers half its question; and the teacher role reads as half-finished.

The four that matter most

1 · v1 distribution needs a staff per-student "download all" BlockerR4-D00 · reframed for the v1 pivot

No student login in v1 → delivery is staff-mediated: a teacher/admin downloads a student's photos and shares them (WhatsApp). The gap: staff can download a single photo or an event's whole gallery, but not "every photo of student X across all their events" in one zip — the exact thing a student could do for themselves. Closing it is small + FE-only (reuses GET /students/{id}/media + useDownloadAll, both roles already entitled). Outbound email is parked (BP12). → BP26 (v1)

2 · The bulk affordances all stop one step short HighR4-A04/05/06/07/13

Bulk student import but no bulk delete/disable; single credential resend but no cohort resend; bulk photo enroll but no retry-failed in that dialog (the event uploader has one); bulk event archive but no bulk staff invite; "select all" acts on the loaded page only. At 800 students / 20 teachers, routine ops become click-marathons. → BP27

3 · The audit log answers half its question; governance actions aren't recorded at all HighR4-A24/25/26

The access log is chronological-only with no filter UI (though the backend event/student filters are already wired — BP8b/BP23) and no CSV export; and there is no admin-action audit anywhere — who disabled, deleted, re-enrolled, or re-invited a student is unrecorded. For a product handling minors' photos, an actor trail is procurement table-stakes. → BP28

4 · The teacher role is under-finished HighR4-T01/02/03/04

An un-delegated teacher gets the full 800-row school with no explanation that delegation exists; the onboarding checklist offers them an "Add a teacher" step that RoleGate-redirects them home; a delegated teacher can filter by their class but can't see its roster; and there's no "what's mine to do" lens. The role works, but reads as half-built. → BP29

Verified, not vibes

Every finding was read from source and reconciled against shipped state. Two exploration claims were corrected during synthesis: the event staleness threshold is 30 minutes (not 60 seconds — no "false escalation" bug), and BP18–BP24 are shipped (a context pass mislabeled them "approved, not built"). Findings already closed by BP18–25 (URL state, category-color collision, stranded-event visibility, reference face in review, honest copy) are not re-filed; the ~11 Round-3 refuted leads stay refuted. The gaps here are real and net-new.

Roadmap — tiered by release-criticality (owner picks)

Minimum viable release  BP26 + BP27 + BP28  — BP26 (a small FE-only staff per-student download) makes v1 distribution work; bulk parity + governance make a school able to run and be accountable. BP29 is close behind (teachers are half the staff base). BP30/BP31 are safe fast-follows. Build BP26 first — it's now cheap (reuses useDownloadAll); outbound email is parked to BP12.
